What you can put on a screen

Every part, and who it is actually for.

A Nimrod screen is made of parts you choose. This is all of them — what each one does for the person it is set up for, what it needs from you, and whether they can use it without being able to press anything.

Start here: can they press anything?

This is the question that decides most of the page, and it is worth being blunt about. Nimrod was built for somebody who cannot reach for a screen, and roughly half of what follows needs no input at all. If the person you are setting this up for cannot press a button, the first group below is the whole product, and that is not a compromise — it is what it was made for.

    This is your profile. Setting it up is optional.

    It is a screen of your own: a room behind it, your picture on the wall and your name on a sign. Change it if you like, or skip it and go straight to anything else. It is always here under Modules, and nothing is made until you press Save.
