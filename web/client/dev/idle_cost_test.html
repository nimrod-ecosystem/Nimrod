What keeps drawing? Each module's cost while nobody touches it

Why (2026-11-02): a kiosk Pi was found running Chromium at about two of its four cores for 31 hours with nobody touching it. A screen that draws all the time has nothing spare for a call. This page mounts every module once, leaves it alone, and counts what it keeps doing: animation frames asked for each second, animations that never end, and timers that fire faster than four times a second.

A report, not a verdict. Some modules move on purpose (a game, a clock's seconds). The question is which ones draw when nothing on them is changing, so the bench knows where to look first. The only checks are that the meter reads a known quantity right and that modules were actually measured.
