archive — the Internet Archive as a place the site plays from (row 2.69)

An archive.org address or an item's name is read through the Archive's public metadata, and its plain sound and video files play in this page's own player (the audio bus, the chosen speaker, Play / Pause / Next). The Archive's own player (its embed frame) is used only for books and, when turned on, games. Only the item names are saved. The metadata fetch is stubbed throughout: nothing here touches the internet.
