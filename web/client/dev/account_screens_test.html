account_screens — the screens on an account

Every screen added with a code, on My dashboards → Devices: its name, when it was last heard from in plain words, when it was added, Rename, and Remove (two presses, no modal). The row for the screen the page is open on says "(this one)". A screen with no sign-in may look but not change. The last section runs against the real server (dev mode): pairs a screen, renames it, and removes it.
