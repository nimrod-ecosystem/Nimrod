the call transport

A screen must never be stuck showing a dead call — she cannot dismiss it. Every path out of a call is asserted here, including the ones nobody chose.

The validated call was iceServers: [] — Tailscale host candidates and nothing else, which is why it only worked over a VPN. That was a shortcut to prove the media path, not a design decision. This fills the array, and rides the signalling on the drive socket that already exists rather than opening a second one.

Not covered here, and it is the important half. No real RTCPeerConnection, no real network, no NAT. This suite proves the STATE MACHINE and the signalling rules — who may send what, what happens when a call drops, what happens when nobody hangs up. Whether two real devices on real networks actually connect is a bench test with two machines, and it has not been run. Neither has anything through a TURN relay, because none is configured.
