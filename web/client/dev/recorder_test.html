the paired recorder

A wrong WAV header has exactly the same structure as a right one.

That is the QR lesson from AGENTS.md wearing a different hat, so the WAV writer is not checked against my reading of the spec — it is decoded back by the browser's own decodeAudioData, which is a genuinely independent implementation, and the samples are compared to what went in. A header that passes a field-by-field check and produces a file nothing can open is exactly the failure that suite was written about.

Everything else runs with no microphone and no audio: capture and the clock are injected, so the sequencing, the alignment and the manifest are exercised as arithmetic.
