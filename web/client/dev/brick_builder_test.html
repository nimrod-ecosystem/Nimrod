brick_builder — make a model from Nimrod parts

Mike, 2026-10-09: "We aren't limited to A1 sizes now that it's for the site. ... We need some sort of builder on the site for making models out of the bricks." This checks the model (parts on the 40 mm grid, turning, things falling onto what is under them), every move of the editor (put, pick up, turn, paint, take away, undo and redo, the hand's part, size and colour, the floor), that no size is capped at a printer's bed, the drawing (well formed, back to front, the cursor and the part in your hand), where a press lands, the match Build it uses, files in and out (the published desk's own file too), and the Brick builder on a page: saved per person, opened again, every move reachable by a switch and by keys. Needs the server (it fetches the published desk).
