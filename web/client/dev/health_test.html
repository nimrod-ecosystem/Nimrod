health — is this panel actually broken?

The hard part is not errors, it is silence. A module that throws is easy and rare. What happens at a bedside is a panel that is up, drawing, apparently fine, and has not changed in forty minutes. Nothing threw. From across the room it looks like a working screen.

False positives are the only failure mode that matters. A missed fault costs an afternoon of a broken panel. A false one swaps away something she was watching, or reboots a screen that was fine — and destroys trust in every alert after it. Every default here under-reports.
