stackline — row 2.76 (brick games, 2026-10-10)

Chat's idea for Mike's "Any other ideas for games with our bricks?": stack to the line. This suite checks the rules on their own (stack_line.js): the levels get harder in order, every size and speed can be set, a dropped brick lands on a whole cell by default so what is cut off is a whole brick, a miss costs nothing by default, and reaching the line starts a fresh tower. Then the panel (modules/stackline.js): a tower built to the line with one switch and nothing else, every other input as the same press, the theme's colours, the play state told to the shell, the demo that never scores, and a teardown that leaves nothing running. Time is stepped through an injected clock.
