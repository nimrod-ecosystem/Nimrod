dwell — holding still is the click

Without the re-arm rule, somebody who is still gets a click every 1.8 seconds forever — a screen operated at random by a person who has stopped moving. That is the section marked with stars, and it is the reason this file exists.

Found by auditing the private cici_color.js against this repo's input_marker.js. The detection half had ported faithfully; the ACTIVATION half had not come across at all, so the public colour tracker could point at things and never select one. These are the private build's own numbers — 1800 ms, 70 px, a 1.5× re-arm — not fresh guesses.

Not covered here. Anything with a real camera or a real person: whether 1800 ms is right for anybody but the one person it was tuned for, and whether 70 px of wander matches what a colour marker on a foot actually produces on a Pi 400. Those are bench questions. This suite is the state machine and the wiring.
