board clips into a call (call loadouts, 2026-10-09)

A board card whose word has a recorded clip (aac/audio, Piper) sends that clip INTO the call's outgoing sound (call_mix.js: microphone + clip into one track, swapped onto the call's audio sender with replaceTrack), and its text says so, so the far end shows it and does not read it out a second time. Checked here: the clip rule's one home; the mixer with a fake audio context (a missing, slow or refused clip leaves the call untouched; the room microphone is held while the clip plays and comes back after; a second word reuses the mix; a muted microphone stays muted); the transport (order kept, nothing from an ended call); the board hands the clip; the caller page does not read a word it already heard. The last section uses this browser's REAL audio and a REAL peer connection on this machine, and measures the sound arriving at the other end.
