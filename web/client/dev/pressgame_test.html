pressgame — the go/no-go loop, and what it writes down

The graphics are a day's work; the data model is the job. Most of what follows is about the record rather than the game: that a hold and the hold that was asked for never collapse into one number, that an echo press after the win is perseveration and not a false alarm, that a stalled frame is recorded as the machine's fault rather than hers, and that nothing here adds up to a score. Driven by a simulation clock, one frame at a time — no requestAnimationFrame, so it runs the same in a hidden pane.
