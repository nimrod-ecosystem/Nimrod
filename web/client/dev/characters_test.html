characters, world kits and the town (Claude Design, 2026-10-12)

Design's characters handoff ships three files as they are: character_kit.js (a full-body character from a look, an outfit, a role and a pose), theme_kits.js (each world's furniture as SVG) and town.js (each theme's eight places, as room recipes, and a flat drawing of each). This suite checks those files are Design's, byte for byte; that EVERY theme draws every character, piece and place, each with a label; that NO drawing anywhere carries a script, an event handler or a reference outside itself; and the three places the site uses them: the avatar maker's full-body character (avatar_figure.js, kept as data), the room builder's piece list (room_parts.js: each world's pieces drawn in a real room) and the cards (their own suite, playing_cards_test.html).
