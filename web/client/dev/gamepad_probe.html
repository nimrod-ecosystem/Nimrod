gamepad probe

Plug a controller in and press something on it — a browser hides gamepads until the first input arrives, so nothing shows up until you do. An Xbox Adaptive Controller counts, and so does any switch plugged into one of its jacks. This runs the real input_gamepad.js and input.js, not a demo copy, so whatever it shows here is what the binder will see.
