playing cards (Claude Design, 2026-10-10)

playing_cards.js is Design's file, shipped as it is: cardSvg(card, theme, options) draws any card in any of Design's 21 decks. This suite checks that file (unchanged, every card in every deck, the labels, the suit colours measured on every face) and card_deck.js, the site's side of it: which deck each site theme wears, the two settings, the drawing made safe to repeat on a page, and the theme followed as it changes. The games that use it (solitaire, card sort) have their own suites.
