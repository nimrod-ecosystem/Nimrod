playing cards (Claude Design, 2026-10-10; characters and the town, 2026-10-12)

playing_cards.js is Design's file, shipped as it is: cardSvg(card, theme, options) draws any card in any of Design's 21 decks. Since the characters handoff it draws the court cards as Design's four characters standing in their homes, and every card with its suit's place in the theme's town behind it (character_kit.js and town.js, also Design's, also as they are). This suite checks that file (unchanged, every card in every deck, the labels, the suit colours, the contrast of every corner index and pip MEASURED over the scene at the kiosk's smallest card) and card_deck.js, the site's side of it: which deck each site theme wears, the three settings, the town seam, the drawing made safe to repeat on a page, and the theme followed as it changes. The games that use it (solitaire, card sort) have their own suites.
