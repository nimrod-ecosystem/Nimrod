mixer — row 2.35

The audio bus does the arithmetic (faders, minimums, master — see audio_bus_test). This page tests the other two halves: the settings that drive it (mixer.js) and the effects (mixer_fx.js): a per-channel insert chain with a compressor, a scene-matched reverb and a plugin slot. The graph is checked against a fake that traces connections, and then rendered for real in an OfflineAudioContext, because "a failing effect is bypassed, never silencing" is a claim about sound, not about wiring.
