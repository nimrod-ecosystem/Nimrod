recovery — what to do when a panel stops working

Pure, at a fake clock. Nothing reboots, nothing mounts — which is exactly what lets the frightening half of the ladder be walked exhaustively. The actuator is deliberately not here and cannot be: a browser cannot reboot a Pi. This layer decides; something else acts.

The ladder is swap → reload → reboot → notify. Each rung is more disruptive and less available than the one before it: a swap is invisible, a reload costs two seconds and works on every device, a reboot needs hardware you own, and notifying spends a person's attention.
