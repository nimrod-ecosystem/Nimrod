recovery — what to do when a panel stops working

Pure, at a fake clock. Nothing reboots, nothing mounts — which is exactly what lets the frightening half of the ladder be walked exhaustively. The actuator is deliberately not here and cannot be: a browser cannot reboot a Pi. This layer decides; something else acts.

The ladder is remount → reload → swap → reboot → notify, and each rung widens in turn: one panel, then the page, then giving up on the content, then the machine, then a person's attention. The swap sits fourth because it is a concession — everything before it is an attempt to give her back the thing she was actually watching.
