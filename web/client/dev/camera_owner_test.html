camera owner — the arbiter for a device that cannot be shared

The speaker can be summed, so the audio bus ducks. A camera cannot — on Linux a second getUserMedia on the same webcam fails or steals it. So this is a strict single owner. The two things wanting that one capture are a screen and a network, not two panels: during a call the mirror is the self-view, and the outgoing track is what the caller sees. So the clone exists so the peer connection can stop its track at hangup without stopping the capture the picture-in-picture is still showing. No camera and no clock — both are injected.
