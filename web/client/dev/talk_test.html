talk.html — the board on its own

A board must never be the thing someone uses to summon help. Nothing on this page or in talk.js sends anything anywhere: selections speak in the room, and the log of what was chosen stays in this browser. The last section asserts it.

The page at /talk.html is the communication board with nothing in front of it — no sign-in, no profile, no screen to compose first. These checks cover the parts that were added to make that work: the grid that turns when the screen shape would make the cards into stripes, the aim that lets a dwell or a remote choose a card, and the settings this page keeps on the device.

Not covered here. Whether a Fire tablet has a speech voice — that is a property of a device, not of this code, and it is the reason sayBanner defaults on. Also not covered: whether the sixteen words on the care board are the right sixteen for anybody but the person they were assembled for.
