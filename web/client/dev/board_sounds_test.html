a board card's own sound (own board clips, 2026-10-09)

A person's own board can have its own sounds: "Record this word", "Use a sound file", "Remove the sound" in the board editor. The sound is kept on this device and in the person's Nimrod folder ("Board sounds"), never on our server; the card plays its own sound first, then the shipped clip by the naming rule, then the browser's voice; and the same sound goes into a call through the call mixer. Every check here runs on fakes - a fake recorder, fake folders, a fake speaker - and the suite counts every request it makes.
