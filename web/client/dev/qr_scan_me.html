Point a phone at these

Everything in qr_test.html proves these are correct QR codes — the bytes are right, the symbol reads back as the string that went in. None of it proves a camera can see one. Contrast, module size, screen glare and focus live outside any test that runs in a browser.

So: open your phone's camera and point it at each square. It should offer to open the URL printed underneath. You do not have to tap it — the right URL appearing is the whole test.

Level Q is what the pairing screen uses, at roughly this size. M and H are here to compare against. If Q reads instantly and the others hesitate, that is worth knowing before a screen goes to a facility.

The real thing, and two neighbors

How small is too small?

The pairing screen sizes its QR between 220 and 380 px. This row walks down past that floor on purpose: find the point where your phone stops reading, and check the floor is comfortably above it. A phone held by somebody with unsteady hands needs more margin than one on a desk.
