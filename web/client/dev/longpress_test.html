long press — hold a switch for the plain bar

Design (room-is-the-screen, "The plain bar"): a long switch press, 1.5 s by default and settable from 1 to 3 s, summons the plain bar; a fill ring appears after 250 ms saying "Keep holding for the plain bar". A short press is an ordinary press. Driven here with a hand-turned clock and the input bus's own physical-edge records (access/edge), so nothing waits for real.
