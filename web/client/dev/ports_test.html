ports — a module's declared ports, over the bus

No server needed. ports.js is the helper a module uses to SEND on its declared OUT ports and LISTEN on its declared IN ports, without changing the ctx contract: it takes the rootBus and instanceId a module already receives, plus its own manifest. A port that was not declared, or is declared in the other direction, is refused quietly rather than thrown, so one module's mistake cannot take a host down.
