the microphone arbiter

Two consumers of a live microphone is a privacy event, not a resource conflict.

The twin of camera_owner.js — one device, ref-counted, one owner — with a problem the camera never had: the settings that make a call sound good are the wrong ones for recognition. A call wants echo cancellation and noise suppression on; a recogniser wants them off, because suppression is tuned to keep confident typical speech and discard the rest, which is a fair description of exactly the articulation it most needs to hear.

One stream has one set of constraints, so both cannot be satisfied. This opens raw and tells a consumer when the live stream is not what it asked for — because you can always degrade a clean signal and you can never restore a processed one, and because silence is the one answer that is never right here.

No microphone is used anywhere in this suite. getUserMedia and enumerateDevices are both injected.
