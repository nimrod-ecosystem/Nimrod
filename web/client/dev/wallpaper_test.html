the live wallpaper

A full-screen animation running unattended for hours, in front of somebody with a brain injury, is where photosensitivity actually bites. The first section is the only one that matters if you only read one.

A held screen shows a frozen frame and a ⏸ chip today. A stopped picture of a face reads as a broken screen, and this one sits in a room for hours. So: the video modules publish held/begin, the container raises a wallpaper over the stage, and the paused segment underneath is not touched — which is the assertion this suite exists for second, after the flash rate.

Not covered here, deliberately. The media path — a real folder of pictures or video resolved through the media agent — is exercised only through its pure parts (usableItems, nextItem, wallpaperMode). Mounting the module against a live agent is what personal_test and photos_test do for their own modules and it is not repeated here, so a wallpaper actually rendering somebody's video has not been run. Neither has anything on real hardware: what a Pi 400 does with a looping video behind a paused one is a bench question, not a browser one.
