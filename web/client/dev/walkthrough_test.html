A recording of a real bedside instance would put somebody's photographs, voice clips and name on a public landing page. The data gate is the first section, and it is the only one that cannot be fixed after the fact.

One list, five consumers: the recorder performs it, Piper narrates it, the captions and the transcript are made of it, and the guided tour walks a person through it instead of doing it for them. The recorder executes the list, so a stale selector fails the recording — which makes the video run a test of the tour.

Not covered here. No recorder exists yet — the spec says write the step list first and get it reviewed as text, because it is the part that gets revised most. So nothing below drives a browser, and no selector has been proven against the live pages by this suite; they were checked by hand on 2026-09-01. The recorder is what will keep them honest.
