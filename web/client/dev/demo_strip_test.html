Signed out, kiosk.html is the front door — the landing page's loudest button goes straight there. A visitor lands on a working screen full of sample photos, and nothing used to say so, or mention that their own photos could be on it in ten seconds with nothing installed and nothing uploaded. The folder picker had been built and shipped for weeks and was never offered anywhere a first-time visitor would find it.

Why this is its own module and not ten lines in kiosk.html: the signed-out branch cannot be reached in local development at all — the dev server answers /api/me with a stub user, so that code never runs. Without a seam this would have been eyeballed once on production and never checked again.
