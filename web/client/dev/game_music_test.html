game music — the bed, and what happens when the folder is not there

Cici's version streamed tracks off the external drive. That mechanism could not come across — no drive, no manifest — and "so it has no music" was the wrong conclusion. There are two sources now: a linked folder, exactly like photos, and a synthesised hum that needs nothing, so a game has music the first time anybody opens it. Most of what follows is about the second one covering for the first, with a reason attached — silence is indistinguishable from broken, and the person in front of it cannot tell you which. No audio hardware and no network: the context and the audio element are injected.
