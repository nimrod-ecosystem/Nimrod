a coloured marker, as an input

A pose model could not find her foot. A brightly coloured sock is found by arithmetic.

MediaPipe Pose was tried first on the bedside build and failed in the room: drop foot gives atypical geometry, BlazePose's foot landmarks are its weakest, and a pale foot on a white sheet has almost no contrast. The fix was not a better model — it was put a marker on the person. That turns recognition into a per-pixel threshold and a centroid: about 30 fps on a Pi 400 against roughly 6 for hand landmarks, and no model file to ship, download or point at a folder.

No camera is used anywhere in this suite. Every frame below is built by hand as an array, which is what makes the detector testable at all — and it is also the seam a calibration panel and a future landmark detector plug into. What is not covered here is therefore stated plainly at the bottom: real optics, real lighting, and a real sock.
