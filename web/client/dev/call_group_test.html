group calls (2026-10-09)

Mike: "So for the group calls they'd need their own VPN or something?" No - a small call is a mesh: each person holds one connection per other person, set up through the site's socket, which carries only the setting-up. Checked here, with fake connections and a fake server that routes group messages the way drive.py CallGroups does: who offers to whom (the member who was there first; never to somebody already connected by the call that rang); a screen holds its answers until its call is live; a talk-board word and a board clip reach every other person; a tile per person; leaving cleans up; the screen's call outlives the caller who rang while others stay; the caller page adds a screen, lets a knock in, carries on when the first screen leaves, and offers "Ask to join" after a busy; the screen's Call panel draws a tile per person. The server's own rules are tested in web/server/test_call_groups.py.
