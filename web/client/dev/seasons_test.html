seasons and the sky

Mike, 2026-10-05: a theme you can set to follow the seasons (and holidays, starting with Halloween), and a live wallpaper that shows the time of day and the weather where you are. Then Claude Design's seasons-holidays handoff the same day: thirteen scenes (Spring, Summer and a scene per holiday), their palettes, and theme_schedule.js, which is now the one place that decides which look shows. This checks the schedule's dates for every holiday in 2026-2028 (Easter and Thanksgiving computed, Hanukkah and Lunar New Year from the browser's calendars, Diwali from its checked table), the per-holiday ticks, the "why" and "coming up" sentences, the change waiting for a calm moment and fading in, the scenes themselves (Hanukkah's candles, fireworks that bloom and go soft with the photosensitivity setting, nothing moving without motion), the time of day and the weather, and the rows on a real screen.
