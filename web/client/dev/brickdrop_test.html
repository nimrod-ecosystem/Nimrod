brickdrop — row 2.76 (brick games)

"Maybe a Tetris with our bricks." This suite checks the rules on their own (brick_drop.js): the pieces are our bricks (every part is in bricks.json, and no piece is one of the seven four-square shapes), the levels get harder in order, a full row is built and goes, and at the easy levels a full wall starts a fresh one instead of ending the game. Then the panel (modules/brickdrop.js): a row built with one switch and nothing else (the glide, and the scan), keys and the brick falling, the theme's colours on the bricks, the play state told to the shell, the demo that never scores, and a teardown that leaves nothing running. Time is stepped through an injected clock.
