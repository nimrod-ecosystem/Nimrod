Mike, 2026-10-09: "Can the subtitles learn, if I correct what they're saying? Like 'What I/they said was...'" A correction by voice or by hand replaces the line; the words it missed and the "heard X, meant Y" fixes are kept per person on this device, sent to the recognisers that can use them, and attached to a voice recording only when that person records. Nothing reaches the server.
