The Nimrod game

Step 1: your profile

Your profile is your first dashboard: a screen that is yours, with a room behind it, your picture on the wall and your name on a sign. These buttons build it for real. Every one of them is safe to press again: it finds what it already made instead of making another, and it leaves the rest of your screens alone.

  1. Make my profile An empty screen called “My profile”, with a room as its background.
  2. Hang your picture A picture frame on the wall. You choose the picture yourself, in its settings.
  3. Put up your name sign A sign with your name on it.
  4. Open my profile Your profile, on the real screen, with the bar along the bottom.
    Open it

Two things to try there

  • Pick your room. Press ⚙ on the bar (or M), then press Colours until you find a room you like. The live ones move.
  • Make the sign yours. Press Panel ▸ on the bar until the sign has the outline around it, press ⚙, and change its Words, its Font and the Colour of the words. The picture works the same way: select it, then choose Picture and Frame.
