Honest, on purpose

Built. Planned. Hoped.

Every claim on this site fits one of three buckets, and this page says which. "Built" means you can click it right now. "Planned" is real work that isn't done. "Hoped" is an idea nobody has committed to yet.

Built

  • Five course pages, each with a short and long outline, documentation, and a live "try it" link into the real software.
  • A proposed program calendar as an editable content pack.
  • The library dashboard. It is a real page you can set as the home screen of an old device. It has a clock, a quote and a fact that change, and games that start with one tap.
  • Cleveland Museum of Art Open Access images, downloaded and verified CC0, stored locally so the demo works offline.
  • A sample screen to try (Pictures, live view, Word Forge, Clock). If the site is slow to answer, it waits and tries again. It never shows a raw error page.
  • Homeschool and Print Your Own pages (the latter's actual parts are still in design — see below).

Planned

  • More talk board courses after the first one, going deeper into choosing words. (A talk board is also called an AAC board. AAC is augmentative and alternative communication: ways to say something when speaking is hard.)
  • A group chat linked to class events, for people who choose to join.
  • Upload-your-own AAC boards, and using a board to control other things in the room.
  • Bingo played with only a switch (one big button). The screen steps through the choices one at a time, and you press when yours is lit.
  • A Programs part that reads the library's own calendar, so the list is no longer edited by hand.
  • The three physical parts on Print Your Own (bookends, bookshelf, book-carrying case) — designed, not yet built.

Hoped

  • A private, local AI running on the machine in front of you — Course D's whole premise, not yet real.
  • A page of checked links about 3D printing, and about open-source talk boards (AAC) and assistive technology.
  • Calibration that reads a switch's own timing data back into a suggested setting, instead of hand-tuning.

How this was made

Made with the help of AI (artificial intelligence), with a person making every decision.

This site, and the Nimrod software it is built on, was made with the help of AI (Claude). Mike made the design decisions and the reasons behind them. The AI did not. He is also building a private AI of his own that runs on his own computer. That is what Course D on this site describes.
