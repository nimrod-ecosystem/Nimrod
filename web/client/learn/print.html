Makerspace & fabrication

Print Your Own

The basic Nimrod bricks. Every brick is a multiple of 40 mm in every direction, with a 10 mm socket in the middle of every 40 mm square on every face, so any brick lines up with any other. The brackets and plates that join them use the same sockets. Every part comes from one parametric build123d script, so a new size is a number, not a new model.

What "preferred format" means here

The STL is the output. The build123d script is the design.

An STL is a mesh — good for printing, useless for changing a dimension or adding a feature. Every part here is generated from one build123d Python script, so a brick of a size not listed here, or a longer bracket, is editing a number, not re-modeling a shape from scratch.
