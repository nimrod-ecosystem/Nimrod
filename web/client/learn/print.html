Makerspace & fabrication

Print Your Own

A modular parts library built from Nimrod's brick connector system — bookends, a small bookshelf, and a book-carrying case, all one system of interchangeable parts. Every part ships with its build123d source beside the STL: the parametric script that generated it, which is what a makerspace actually needs to change a design rather than just reprint it.

Status, honestly

This page is the template — layout, download flow, and instructions format — built ahead of the parts themselves. The bookends, bookshelf, and book-carrying case are in design now; nothing below is downloadable yet, and this page says so per part rather than showing an empty gallery. Parts appear here the moment they're ready, by updating parts.json — no page rewrite.

What "preferred format" means here

The STL is the output. The build123d script is the design.

An STL is a mesh — good for printing, useless for changing a dimension or adding a feature. Every part here is generated from a build123d Python script, published beside its STL, so resizing a shelf or widening a bookend for a different book size is editing a number, not re-modeling a shape from scratch.
