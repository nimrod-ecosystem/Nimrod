Course D · Coming soon

Build Your Own Local AI

A syllabus, not a working course. Nimrod's long-term plan is one adapter that can speak to several AI backends, with a model that runs on the machine in front of you as the free, private default. This page describes that plan honestly: what's built, what isn't, and what this course will teach once it exists.

Not built yet Adults Planned: 60 min

Nothing on this page is running software. The adapter and the local-model integration are on Nimrod's roadmap, not in the product. This course will be scheduled once they ship.

Planned outline

What this course will cover.

  1. Why "local" matters here: any AI that touches private communication or care information runs on the person's own machine, not a vendor's server — the same privacy line the rest of the platform draws for cameras and files.
  2. What a small AI (artificial intelligence) model on your own computer can and cannot do today, on ordinary equipment. That means the same Raspberry Pi (a very small computer) or laptop the rest of Nimrod runs on. It does not mean a powerful computer with its own graphics card.
  3. Installing and running a program that serves an AI model on your own computer (a tool like Ollama), and telling Nimrod where to find it. That is 1 line of settings, on purpose.
  4. A worked example: a suggestion feature that offers a phrase back to someone using an AAC board, entirely on-device.
  5. The other choice: bring your own key (a code from an AI service that lets you use it). This is for anyone who wants a more capable model that runs on a company's computers, and who accepts that trade-off knowingly. It is a choice, not the default.

Try it
